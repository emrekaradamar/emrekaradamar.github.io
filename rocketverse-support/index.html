Frequently Asked Questions

The game is not working as expected. What should I do?

First, make sure your device is running a supported operating system and that Rocketverse is updated to the latest version. If the issue continues, contact us with the steps that reproduce it.

I found a bug or have a suggestion.

We appreciate your feedback. Send us a description of what happened, and include a screenshot or screen recording if it helps explain the issue.

I have a question about ads or privacy.

For questions about advertising, data use, or privacy, please contact us using the email address above.

My game progress or settings seem to be missing.

Please contact support before reinstalling the game or clearing its data. Include your device details and explain when you first noticed the issue.
